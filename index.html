Privacy Policy

App: EZ Timer (package: com.tt.ez_timer_stopwatch)
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the EZ Timer Android app (“the app”) handles. The app is a timer and stopwatch. It is provided by TT (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information on any server of ours. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers operated by us.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences on your phone only, such as the timer duration, delay, beep interval, text-to-speech option, digit color, and the last selected tab. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version);
  • information about how you interact with ads.

We do not receive your personal information from this process. For details on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy laws, the app may show a consent message (provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform) asking for your choices about personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time using the “Privacy settings” button in the app’s Settings, where it is available.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: to load ads.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the timer and stopwatch running, and to play beeps or spoken announcements while the screen is off. The notification contains no personal data.
  • Wake lock: to keep the timer accurate while the screen is off.

Spoken announcements use your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio and does not access your microphone, contacts, camera, photos, or precise location.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we hold no personal data about you to access, correct, or delete. For data handled by Google through advertising, you can use the controls described in sections 3 and 4 and Google’s own privacy tools.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date above updated.
